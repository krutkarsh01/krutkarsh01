### [v2.1.0] - 2026-11-04: Mobile Responsiveness Remediation: Eliminating Portfolio Card Overlap & Streamlining Mobile Navigation Drawer

#### 1. Root Cause Analysis of User Reported Bugs

##### A. Defect 2.1: Portfolio Card Overlap on Mobile (`#portfolio-curved-gallery`)
- **Root Cause**:
  - In `index.html`, the 3D cylindrical carousel rotates 20 cards around an imaginary cylinder with radius `R = 470px` and angular step `step = 18°`.
  - On a 375px mobile viewport, each card is styled with `width: clamp(220px, 62vw, 248px) !important` (232px wide).
  - Flanking cards at `±18°` are positioned at `x = ±145px`.
  - Because `232px / 2 = 116px`, the center card extends from `-116px` to `+116px`.
  - The right flanking card at `+145px` extends from `+29px` to `+261px`.
  - **Intersection**: The right card physically overlaps the center card by `116px - 29px = 87 pixels`!
  - Furthermore, `updateCards()` set flanking card opacity to `0.30 - 0.70`, meaning the neighboring cards were semi-transparent and visibly intersecting directly behind and through the active card.
- **Architectural Fix**:
  - On mobile (`isMobile === true` / `< 600px` / `< 768px`):
    - Enforce **Strict Single-Card Focus**: When `dist < 8°`, `opacity = 1; visibility: visible; pointer-events: auto`.
    - When `dist >= 8°`, immediately set `opacity = 0; visibility: hidden; pointer-events: none`.
    - Result: Flanking cards are completely hidden from the viewport on mobile, eliminating all card collisions. When the user swipes or taps arrows, the transition is instantaneous and clean with 1 card active at a time.
    - On tablet and desktop (`w >= 768px`), the wide multi-card 3D curved cylinder continues to display flanking cards gracefully without collision.

##### B. Defect 2.2: Mobile Header & Drawer Navigation Overlap
- **Root Cause**:
  - On mobile screens (< 480px / 375px), `.brand-logo` (`[Crest]` 38px + gap 8px + `KUMAR UTKARSH` 115px + `木ノ葉` 44px = 205px) and `.nav-actions` (`[Theme]` 44px + gap 8px + `[Hamburger]` 44px = 96px) combined with `0.4rem 1.4rem` padding totaled `353px`.
  - Inside a 375px viewport with container padding, this exceeded container boundaries, pushing the hamburger button right against and past the right rivet (`•`).
  - Furthermore, `.mobile-nav` was styled with inconsistent `top` coordinates (`top: 62px` vs `top: 72px`), overlapping the bottom 10px of the fixed headband, and had `z-index` conflicts.
- **Architectural Fix**:
  1. **Mobile Headband (< 480px)**:
     - Hide `.brand-sub-badge` (`木ノ葉`) on viewports `< 480px` to save 44px of horizontal space.
     - Reduce `.nav-wrap` lateral padding to `0.4rem 0.85rem !important`.
     - Anchor rivets at `left: 6px` and `right: 6px` with `pointer-events: none`.
     - Result: Entire navbar contents fit within 290px, leaving 50px+ of breathing room on 375px screens with zero rivet collision.
  2. **Mobile Nav Drawer (`#mobile-menu`)**:
     - Enforce `z-index: 9998 !important`, with `nav` at `z-index: 10000 !important` so the headband is always on top.
     - Set `top: 74px !important`, `height: calc(100vh - 74px) !important; max-height: calc(100dvh - 74px) !important`.
     - Enable smooth vertical scrolling: `overflow-y: auto !important; -webkit-overflow-scrolling: touch !important`.
     - Clean typography and single-kanji square badges aligned with chevron arrows.

##### C. Defect 2.3: Prototypes Directory Grid (`/prototypes/`) Mobile Layout
- **Audit**:
  - Verify `.projects-grid` and `.proto-card` on mobile. Ensure strict 1-column layout (`grid-template-columns: 1fr !important; width: 100%; gap: 24px;`) with zero negative margin bleed causing cards to overlap vertically.
